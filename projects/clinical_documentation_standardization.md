# Clinical Documentation Standardization | Template Design

**Type:** Owner-reported historical case, American Medical Staffing.

The owner describes redesigning evaluation, reevaluation, treatment-plan and discharge templates for an agency using an electronic clinical system. Historical résumé reports approximately **35% better documentation performance** and **87% fewer documentation-related errors**. Underlying denominators, measure definitions and observation dates remain to be validated.

## Demonstration deliverables

- Evaluation/documentation completeness checklist.
- Data-quality definitions for missing, late, incomplete and contradictory records.
- Workflow map: author → QA → correction → approval → release.
- Sampling and measurement protocol for before/after error review.

## Limits

No agency templates or real case files are published. A 35% performance improvement and 87% error reduction must not be added together or converted to monetary savings without denominators, rates, rework time and costing.
