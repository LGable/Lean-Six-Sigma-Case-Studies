# Documentation and Billing Reconciliation | Workflow Standardization

**Type:** Owner-reported historical implementation. No real student information included.

## Problem

Responsibilities for occupational therapy documentation review, discrepancy resolution and billing approval were insufficiently explicit, risking rework and confusion between clinical and administrative teams.

## Implementation

1. Defined a structured review and discrepancy log.
2. Coordinated reconciliation covering **46 student service records** (aggregate only).
3. Clarified treating therapist, clinical department lead and administrator responsibilities.
4. Retained **final billing control with administrative personnel**.
5. Transitioned process to ongoing operational ownership.

## Controls and measurement

A public-safe demo can track total records reviewed, discrepancies by category, assigned owner, due date, resolution time, and final administrative sign-off. Actual records, names, payer details or billing extracts are not included.

## Result claim

Documented implementation scope: 46 records reconciled. No statistically demonstrated reduction in error rates or proven monetary savings is asserted.
