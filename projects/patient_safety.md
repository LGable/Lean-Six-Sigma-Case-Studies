# Psychiatric Patient Safety Improvement | Change Implementation

**Type:** Historical professional contribution + fictional visual demonstration.

## Context

On an inpatient pediatric/adolescent psychiatric unit, I was a frontline contributor to CPI-informed de-escalation and implementation practices. Restraint frequency decreased from approximately two events per day to two events per week across my assigned shifts, observed over two months using restraint documentation, patient records, and nursing follow-up reports. This was associated with my de-escalation practices; it does not represent unit-wide performance or establish sole causality.

## Illustrative improvement approach

- Define restraint as a balancing-sensitive safety outcome with clearly stated denominator (e.g., events per 1,000 patient-days).
- Review patient mix and incident severity with clinical safety leadership.
- Standardize frontline de-escalation response, training, and escalation guidance.
- Track implementation fidelity alongside safety measures and operational burdens.
- Use debriefs to seek system-level causes rather than assign individual blame.

`data/safety_run_chart_synthetic.csv` contains **invented monthly incident counts and patient-days** only. It does not reproduce the historical observation or any hospital safety log. See the analysis script for rates and cautions.

## Limitations

No claim of sole causality, formal RCA leadership, independently audited outcome, or documented cost savings is made.
