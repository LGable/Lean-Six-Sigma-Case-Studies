# Psychiatric Patient Safety Improvement | Change Implementation

**Type:** Historical professional contribution + fictional visual demonstration.

## Context

On an inpatient pediatric/adolescent psychiatric unit, the owner reports serving as a frontline implementation lead for CPI-informed de-escalation practices associated with an **approximately 80% restraint-utilization reduction**. Measurement period, case mix, denominators and independent attribution are not established in this portfolio.

## Illustrative improvement approach

- Define restraint as a balancing-sensitive safety outcome with clearly stated denominator (e.g., events per 1,000 patient-days).
- Review patient mix and incident severity with clinical safety leadership.
- Standardize frontline de-escalation response, training, and escalation guidance.
- Track implementation fidelity alongside safety measures and operational burdens.
- Use debriefs to seek system-level causes rather than assign individual blame.

## Demonstration data

`data/safety_run_chart_synthetic.csv` contains **invented monthly incident counts and patient-days** only. It does not reproduce the 80% historical result or any hospital safety log. See the analysis script for rates and cautions.

## Limitations

No claim of sole causality, formal RCA leadership, independently audited outcome, or documented cost savings is made.
